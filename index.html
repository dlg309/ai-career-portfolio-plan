08 / METHOD & DECISIONS

How this record was made.

The original Review 1 role cards and portfolio decision remain above. This method describes what was checked and what still needs research.

I started with FDE, AI platform, and healthcare AI descriptions for the assignment, then added internship examples relevant to May 2028 and a Solutions Engineer example. Project skills and personal eligibility require separate evidence. AI-generated summaries were treated as leads to verify, not as original sources.
